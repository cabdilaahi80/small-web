Our Story

A school built by the community it serves.

Sayid Maxamed School opened its doors with three classrooms and forty students. Today it is one of the region's most trusted institutions — carrying both the Somali national curriculum and a growing English-medium track, so every graduate leaves fluent, capable, and ready.

Accredited under the Puntland Ministry of Education, following the national primary and secondary curriculum.

Our Mission

To deliver disciplined, high-quality education that builds character, critical thinking, and Islamic values — preparing every student for the next stage of life, whether university, vocation, or civic leadership.

Our Vision

To be Bosaso's leading school for academic excellence — a place where every child, regardless of background, is given the tools to lead with knowledge and integrity.
